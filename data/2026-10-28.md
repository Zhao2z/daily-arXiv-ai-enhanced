Main category: cs.SE

Abstract: Early stopping promises to reduce compute costs for autonomous coding agents by terminating unpromising runs early. Across all four settings at the 75k cap (and 216 of 224 tested budget-threshold configurations overall), uninterrupted continuation achieves higher solve rates at lower mean token cost than both restart policies.
