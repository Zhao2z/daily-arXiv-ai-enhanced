### [1] [Who Tests the Testers? Systematic Enumeration and Coverage Audit of LLM Agent Tool Call Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18245)
*Xuan Chen,Lu Yan,Ruqi Zhang,Xiangyu Zhang*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: SafeAudit是一个元审计框架，通过系统化生成测试用例和引入规则抵抗性指标，发现现有基准测试未能覆盖的20%以上不安全行为，揭示当前安全评估的完整性缺陷。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着LLM代理越来越多地通过外部工具行动，其安全性取决于工具调用工作流而非单纯的文本生成。现有基准测试虽然评估了各种环境和风险类别，但无法回答一个根本问题：现有测试套件的完整性如何？即使代理通过了基准测试，哪些不安全的交互模式仍然存在？

Method: 提出SafeAudit元审计框架，包含两个核心贡献：1）基于LLM的枚举器，通过枚举有效的工具调用工作流和多样化的用户场景来系统化生成测试用例；2）引入规则抵抗性指标，这是一种非语义的量化指标，从现有基准中提取紧凑的安全规则，并识别在这些规则下仍然未被覆盖的不安全交互模式。

Result: 在3个基准测试和12个环境中，SafeAudit发现了超过20%的残余不安全行为，这些行为是现有基准测试未能暴露的。随着测试预算的增加，覆盖率呈单调增长趋势。

Conclusion: 研究结果突显了当前安全评估存在显著的完整性缺陷，并表明元审计是基准测试为基础的代理安全测试的必要补充。

Abstract: Large Language Model (LLM) agents increasingly act through external tools, making their safety contingent on tool-call workflows rather than text generation alone. While recent benchmarks evaluate agents across diverse environments and risk categories, a fundamental question remains unanswered: how complete are existing test suites, and what unsafe interaction patterns persist even after an agent passes the benchmark? We propose SafeAudit, a meta-audit framework that addresses this gap through two contributions. First, an LLM-based enumerator that systematically generates test cases by enumerating valid tool-call workflows and diverse user scenarios. Second, we introduce rule-resistance, a non-semantic, quantitative metric that distills compact safety rules from existing benchmarks and identifies unsafe interaction patterns that remain uncovered under those rules. Across 3 benchmarks and 12 environments, SafeAudit uncovers more than 20% residual unsafe behaviors that existing benchmarks fail to expose, with coverage growing monotonically as the testing budget increases. Our results highlight significant completeness gaps in current safety evaluation and motivate meta-auditing as a necessary complement to benchmark-based agent safety testing.

### [2] [Can LLMs Reason Like Automated Theorem Provers for Rust Verification? VCoT-Bench: Evaluating via Verification Chain of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18334)
*Zichen Xie,Wenxi Wang*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: VCoT-Lift框架将底层求解器推理提升为高层可读验证步骤，创建VCoT-Bench基准评估LLMs在Rust程序验证中的理解能力，发现现有模型存在严重脆弱性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前LLMs在辅助Rust安全软件开发时，现有评估方法将验证视为黑盒，仅通过二进制通过/失败结果评估证明提示，无法判断模型是否真正理解验证非平凡Rust代码所需的逻辑推理。

Method: 提出VCoT-Lift框架，将底层求解器推理提升为高层、人类可读的验证步骤，通过显式的验证思维链提供细粒度评估的ground truth。基于此创建VCoT-Bench基准，包含1,988个VCoT完成任务。

Result: 评估10个最先进模型显示严重脆弱性，表明当前LLMs远未达到自动定理证明器展示的推理能力。基准从三个正交维度测量性能：对不同程度缺失证明的鲁棒性、对不同证明类型的能力、对证明位置的敏感性。

Conclusion: 现有LLMs在Rust程序验证中的推理能力存在严重不足，需要更深入理解验证过程，VCoT-Lift和VCoT-Bench为细粒度评估提供了新框架。

Abstract: As Large Language Models (LLMs) increasingly assist secure software development, their ability to meet the rigorous demands of Rust program verification remains unclear. Existing evaluations treat Rust verification as a black box, assessing models only by binary pass or fail outcomes for proof hints. This obscures whether models truly understand the logical deductions required for verifying nontrivial Rust code. To bridge this gap, we introduce VCoT-Lift, a framework that lifts low-level solver reasoning into high-level, human-readable verification steps. By exposing solver-level reasoning as an explicit Verification Chain-of-Thought, VCoT-Lift provides a concrete ground truth for fine-grained evaluation. Leveraging VCoT-Lift, we introduce VCoT-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark of 1,988 VCoT completion tasks for rigorously evaluating LLMs' understanding of the entire verification process. VCoT-Bench measures performance along three orthogonal dimensions: robustness to varying degrees of missing proofs, competence across different proof types, and sensitivity to the proof locations. Evaluation of ten state-of-the-art models reveals severe fragility, indicating that current LLMs fall well short of the reasoning capabilities exhibited by automated theorem provers.

### [3] [Where are the Hidden Gems? Applying Transformer Models for Design Discussion Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18393)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 软件设计讨论广泛存在于各种开发平台中，对软件现代化和重构决策有重要价值。传统机器学习方法受限于标注数据稀缺和标注成本高，而Transformer模型在此领域的跨领域应用尚未充分探索。

Method: 对先前跨领域研究进行概念性复制，扩展使用现代Transformer架构（BERT、RoBERTa、XLNet、LaMini-Flan-T5-77M和ChatGPT-4o-mini）。在Stack Overflow上微调模型，在GitHub工件（pull requests、issues、commits）上评估。同时评估了相似词注入的数据增强方法。

Result: BERT和RoBERTa在跨领域表现出强召回率；XLNet精度更高但召回率较低；ChatGPT-4o-mini获得最高召回率和有竞争力的整体性能；LaMini-Flan-T5-77M作为轻量级替代品提供更强精度但性能不够均衡。相似词注入的数据增强未带来有意义改进。

Design discussions spanning a project's history provide valuable information for informed decision-making, such as refactoring and software modernization. Machine learning techniques have been used to detect design decisions in natural language discussions; however, their effectiveness is limited by the scarcity of labeled data and the high cost of annotation. Prior work adopted cross-domain strategies with traditional classifiers, training on one domain and testing on another. Despite their success, transformer-based models, which often outperform traditional methods, remain largely unexplored in this setting. The goal of this work is to investigate the performance of transformer-based models (i.e., BERT, RoBERTa, XLNet, LaMini-Flan-T5-77M, and ChatGPT-4o-mini) for detecting design-related discussions. To this end, we conduct a conceptual replication of prior cross-domain studies while extending them with modern transformer architectures and addressing methodological issues in earlier work. The models were fine-tuned on Stack Overflow and evaluated on GitHub artifacts (i.e., pull requests, issues, and commits). BERT and RoBERTa show strong recall across domains, while XLNet achieves higher precision but lower recall. ChatGPT-4o-mini yields the highest recall and competitive overall performance, whereas LaMini-Flan-T5-77M provides a lightweight alternative with stronger precision but less balanced performance. We also evaluated similar-word injection for data augmentation, but unlike prior findings, it did not yield meaningful improvements. Overall, these results highlight both the opportunities and trade-offs of using modern language models for detecting design discussion.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 1. 构建包含复杂SQL查询和专家验证注释的综合数据集；2. 在大规模SQL语料上进行持续预训练以增强语法和语义理解；3. 监督微调；4. 引入基于人类反馈的直接偏好优化（DPO），使用偏好损失函数优化输出质量

Abstract: SQL query comprehension is a significant challenge due to complex syntax, diverse join types, and deep nesting. Many queries lack adequate comments, severely hindering code readability, maintainability, and knowledge transfer. Automated SQL comment generation faces two main challenges: limited datasets that inadequately represent complex real-world queries, and Large Language Models' (LLMs) insufficient understanding of SQL-specific semantics. Our empirical analysis shows that even after continual pre-training and supervised fine-tuning, LLMs struggle with complex SQL semantics, yielding inaccurate comments. Next, we perform continual pre-training on a large SQL corpus to enhance the LLM's syntax and semantic understanding, followed by supervised fine-tuning. Finally, we introduce Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) using human feedback. SQL-Commenter utilizes a preference-based loss function to favor preferred outputs, enhancing fine-grained semantic learning and context-dependent quality assessment. Evaluated on the Spider and Bird benchmarks, SQL-Commenter significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines. On average, it surpasses the strongest baseline (Qwen3-14B) by 9.29, 4.99, and 13.23 percentage points on BLEU-4, METEOR, and ROUGE-L, respectively. Moreover, human evaluation demonstrates the superior quality of comments generated by SQL-Commenter in terms of correctness, completeness, and naturalness.

### [7] [Measuring and Exploiting Confirmation Bias in LLM-Assisted Security Code Review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18740)

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: LLM代码审查存在确认偏误，攻击者可通过恶意提示框架降低漏洞检测率，去偏技术可恢复检测能力


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究确认偏误是否影响基于LLM的漏洞检测，以及这种缺陷是否可被软件供应链攻击利用

Method: 进行两项互补研究：研究1通过250个CVE漏洞/补丁对在四种先进模型和五种提示框架条件下量化确认偏误；研究2模拟对抗性拉取请求，评估实际攻击可行性
