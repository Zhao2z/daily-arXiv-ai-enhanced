Method: 提出运行时结构不变量：(1) 零黄金基准（zero-golden）模式，作用于计算样式与几何信息；(2) 双向保守（bidi-conservative）差分模式。两者均在无头浏览器中执行检测。

Result: 在由Bootstrap和Material Components Web带日期修复提交重建的14个真实RTL bug留出基准上，检出13/14（零黄金模式2个，差分模式11个），对任何已修复版本无误报；在实际提交的bootstrap.rtl.css上2个中检出1个。在16个负样本LTR孤岛案例上0假阳性；在四个设计系统、三种RTL架构的43个未修改真实组件上两模式均0假阳性（rule-of-three 95%上界≤7%）。另在实际Bulma 1.0.4中发现一个真实方向缺陷。对比：Stylelint核心规则在Bootstrap的LTR与正确RTL构建上发出相同的1744条警告；伪本地化未检出任何镜像bug；朴素像素镜像差分在调参阈值下F1仅0.87。两模式互补：零黄金是装饰/样式不对称类缺陷的唯一检测器（5/6），但对正确的逻辑border-inline-end会误报；差分模式对该类无效却从不误报，且在块级inset/margin类上实证精确。

Conclusion: 两类模式互补，共同覆盖现有工具无法定位的RTL镜像缺陷；本文范围限于检测，修复为未来工作。

Abstract: Bidirectional (right-to-left, RTL) layout defects arise when interfaces authored with physical directional CSS (margin-left, border-left, text-align:left) are rendered under dir="rtl" without being mirrored. Accessibility engines (axe-core), actionability engines (Playwright), and even Stylelint's current core logical-property rule fail to localise these defects. We present runtime structural invariants -- a zero-golden mode over computed style and geometry, and a bidi-conservative differential mode -- that catch them in a headless browser. On a held-out benchmark of 14 real RTL bugs reconstructed from dated fix commits in Bootstrap and Material Components Web, the detector recovers 13/14 (2 via zero-golden, 11 via the differential mode), with no false alarm on any patched version; on the actual committed historical bootstrap.rtl.css it catches 1 of 2 attempted. It reports 0 false positives on 16 negative LTR-island cases and, in both modes, on 43 unmodified real components across four design systems and three RTL architectures (rule-of-three 95% upper bound <= 7%), and it surfaced a real directional defect in the current Bulma 1.0.4. By contrast, Stylelint's core rule emits an identical 1744 warnings on Bootstrap's LTR and correct-RTL builds, pseudo-localisation detects none of the mirroring bugs, and a naive pixel mirror-and-diff reaches only F1 0.87 with a test-set-tuned threshold. The two modes are complementary: zero-golden is the sole detector of the decoration/styling-asymmetry class (5/6) but false-flags the correct logical border-inline-end, while the differential detects none of that class yet never false-alarms and is empirically exact on the block-level inset/margin class. We scope this paper to detection; repair is future work.
